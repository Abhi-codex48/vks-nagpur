Our Story

A Multi-Disciplinary
CA Firm Since 2014

VK&S — Vijaywargi Khabiya & Saoji is a multi-disciplinary chartered accountancy firm based in Nagpur. Established in 2014, the firm provides audit, taxation, representation and advisory support across practice areas, with assignments involving Nagpur, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad, Indore and Raipur.

Our 80+ member team includes Chartered Accountants, Advocates, IBBI-registered Insolvency Professionals and domain professionals working together to deliver integrated, business-relevant solutions.
