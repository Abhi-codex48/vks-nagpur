About the Firm

Established 2014.
Multi-City Practice.

VK&S — Vijaywargi Khabiya & Saoji is a Nagpur-based multi-disciplinary chartered accountancy firm established in 2014. The firm provides professional support across audit, taxation, representation and advisory work, with assignments involving Nagpur, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad, Indore and Raipur.
