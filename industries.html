Our Approach

Sector Context Matters

Industry context affects GST classification, incentive eligibility, audit procedures, financing documentation and reporting schedules. The firm uses sector context to frame the work plan based on the agreed assignment scope and applicable professional requirements.

This approach helps align documentation, compliance review and reporting support with the way each business operates.

How the sector view is used

01

Map sector-specific tax, audit and reporting requirements before execution.

02

Identify incentive, finance and documentation requirements that may affect the assignment.

03

Prepare sector-specific documentation, accounting schedules and financial workings for the assignment.
